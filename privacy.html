Privacy Policy

Eishha Sah (عيشها صح) · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Eishha Sah («عيشها صح») is a daily Islamic life-coaching short-video channel by Mohamed Abu Al-Ola, published on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.

The Eishha Sah Studio app

"Eishha Sah Studio" is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule the channel's own videos and to read their view, like and comment counts. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app uses

  • With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read public statistics of those videos.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only for the purposes above.
  • The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or any other users.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube features are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Visitors to this website

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies or tracking. It only links to the channel's social media pages.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

Contact

Send a direct message to @eishhasah on Instagram.
